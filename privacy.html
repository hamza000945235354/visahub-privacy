Privacy Policy

VisaHub AI · Effective date: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VisaHub AI ("the app", "we", "us"), published by Visa Update Hubs, collects, uses and protects your information when you use our Android application.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and passport country when you sign up, including through Google Sign-In.
  • Assessment details: information you enter in the AI Assessor, such as destination, visa type, age, education, work experience and English level.
  • Consultation content: messages, voice input transcribed to text, and images you upload during an AI consultation.
  • Purchase information: subscription status. Payments are processed by Google Play; we do not receive or store your card details.
  • Device and usage data: device model, operating system, app version, crash logs and advertising ID, collected by our service providers.

2. How we use your information

  • To show visa guides relevant to your passport country.
  • To provide AI assessments and consultations for Premium members.
  • To manage your account and subscriptions.
  • To show ads to users on the free plan.
  • To fix bugs, improve the app and prevent abuse.

3. AI processing

AI features are powered by a third-party AI model provider (DeepSeek). When you use the AI Assessor or AI Consultant, the text and images you submit are sent to this provider to generate a response. Do not upload documents you are not comfortable sharing. AI answers are general guidance, not legal or immigration advice. Always confirm requirements with the official embassy or government website.

4. Advertising

The free version shows banner, native and video ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising ID and device information to show personalised ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings under Google → Ads. Ad-free and Premium plans remove ads.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Play Services, Google Sign-In and Firebase
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing
  • DeepSeek (AI model provider)

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers listed above, as needed to run the app, or when required by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and all related data at any time by emailing hamzasiddique951@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reasonable measures to protect your information.

9. Children's privacy

VisaHub AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
